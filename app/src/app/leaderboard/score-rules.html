Security credits count on the advisory's publication date. Each person earns points for their highest-valued role once per advisory.

Every credited role receives the same severity multiplier: base role points × severity. Without recorded severity, the multiplier is 1.

Why do some merged PRs show as closed on GitHub?

Most PRs from 2017 to 2025 show as closed, not merged: Angular's merge tooling pushes the commits itself and adds PR Close #N to them. Those count as merged too. Bots are left out. The base weights are the ones of Nuxters, plus review comments, security advisory credits and translations. Unlike Nuxters, no repository counts double, a translation counts once for each person credited on it rather than by segment, and a pull request without a type counts as a chore.
