Why do some merged PRs show as closed on GitHub?

Most PRs from 2017 to 2025 show as closed, not merged: Angular's merge tooling pushes the commits itself and adds PR Close #N to them. Those count as merged too. Bots are left out. The weights are the ones of Nuxters, plus review comments and security reports.
